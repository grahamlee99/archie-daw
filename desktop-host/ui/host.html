User Interface

A skin is only a set of colours: change one and the whole program changes at once, and it is remembered.

Browsing

Looks in your Local Builder output and the standard VST3 folder, in separate processes, so a plug-in that crashes cannot take the program down.

Analyser measures a plug-in in a separate process, so a plug-in that crashes cannot take this program down. Every number is measured; nothing is scored by taste.

Pick a plug-in on a track, or any plug-in in the list, and press Measure.

Latency adjustment ms
The program uses the latency the sound card reports to place a recording against the music. If the reported figure is wrong, recorded audio lands early or late. If recorded audio plays back too late, raise the adjustment; if too early, lower it.
Max audio threads
More threads give more power for many tracks and plug-ins; fewer leave room for other programs. It takes effect the next time the program starts.
Master tune (audio clips only) cent
Retunes recorded and imported sounds, keeping their length. Instruments and plug-ins are not changed: tune those in their own windows. A clip takes a moment to be retuned and plays as before until it is ready. 100 cent is one semitone; double-click for 0.
Render audio using the audio card buffer size
Applies to Export and Bounce. Ticked: the sound is worked out in pieces the size of your sound card's buffer, as when playing, which helps if a plug-in sounds different in an exported file. Unticked (usual): pieces of 512 samples.
Play in background
Ticked: Archie DAW keeps the sound card and keeps playing when you switch to another program. Unticked: when you switch away it stops and lets the sound card go, so another program can use it (useful with ASIO), and takes it back when you return. It never lets go while recording.

Other Controls

Keyboard shortcuts

These are the defaults (Reason's, plus Space, C, 0 and R) and your own changes. A shortcut marked "not built yet" is listed so you can see what is planned; it does nothing until its feature exists. Typing in a box never triggers a shortcut.

Folders

Recordings you make are kept in the recordings folder. A recording stays there until you remove it, even when no clip uses it any more.

Account

Each copy of Archie Drums in the rack connects on its own and shows on the website as its own instrument. This connects all of them at once; the first time, your browser asks you to confirm.

EQ

Drag a dot to set a band: across for the frequency, up and down for the gain. The mouse wheel over a dot changes its Q (how wide it is). Double-click a dot to switch the band on or off. Right-click a dot, a filter, a number or an ON button to automate it, for sweeps. The HP and LP squares along the bottom are the high-pass and low-pass: drag them sideways. The line is the EQ curve and the shaded area is the live spectrum of the sound after the EQ.
